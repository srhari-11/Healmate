About HealMate

🌿 About HealMate About HealMate HealMate is a mental wellness platform designed to help individuals manage stress, track emotions, and build healthy daily habits through simple and supportive digital tools. Our Mission Our mission is to make mental wellness accessible and easy by providing guided meditations, mood tracking, self-care activities, and expert support in one user-friendly platform. What We Offer Daily Wellness Tasks – Simple activities to build positive habits. Guided Meditations – Relaxation sessions to reduce stress. Mood Tracking – Monitor and understand emotional patterns. Expert Support – Connect with mental health professionals. Technology Used HealMate is developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Supabase for backend support.

🌿 Our Mission

To promote emotional strength and self-awareness by providing daily activities, guided meditation, and expert support.

🌿 Our Vision

To create a world where mental wellness is prioritized and support is accessible to everyone.
